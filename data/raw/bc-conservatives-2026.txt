Dear British Columbians,
David Eby’s cynical early election call is all about him. I see this election as an
opportunity for you, and for British Columbia, the province that we all love.
This election is about you, and the opportunity to choose strong leadership
that brings the change we need in these serious times.
After nearly a decade of the NDP, it is hard to name one thing that’s better.
Jobs lost, month after month. Young people priced out of home ownership.
Over a million people without a family doctor. High and rising taxes. Strangers
attacked at random. Opioid overdoses being managed with free hard drugs
instead of treatment. Increasing costs for our seniors. Cancelled Long-Term
Care beds. People dying on waitlists instead of getting the surgery they need.
And the cost of living is growing as incomes are stalled.
If you give me the honour of serving as your Premier, here’s my promise to
every British Columbian. I’ll work to get the economy moving, bring your costs
down, and create good jobs. I’ll fully fund and protect our health care, and
fix the mess David Eby and the NDP have made so that British Columbians
aren’t dying on waitlists or going to closed ERs when they need help. I’ll make
our streets safer. I’ll get more homes built. That’s the job I am asking you to
elect me to do. It’s about you, and making your life easier and better.
I want you to live in a British Columbia that is the envy of the world. I have a
strong team behind me and a plan we’re ready to carry out from day one.
David Eby’s opportunistic election has given us a chance to turn this province
around. Let’s take it. But we need your support to do it, and I’m grateful for it.
Together, we can build a new era of hope and growth in BC.
Lorne Doerkson
Leader, Conservative Party of BC

escalating costs.

and costs are expected to climb beyond $12 billion. There is still no firm
completion date. Billions more, years lost, and commuters and businesses
remain stuck in traffic.
We will bring discipline, predictability and transparency to capital spending. A
Conservative government will:
• 	Conduct a thorough, comprehensive review and audit of the
provincial capital plan, establishing clear priorities, realistic budgets,
delivery schedules and stronger oversight to ensure taxpayers receive
value for money, and report out within six months on its findings and
recommendations.
